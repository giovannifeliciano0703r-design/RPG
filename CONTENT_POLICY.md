# Content policy for maintainers

1. Prefer original homebrew examples or material with a documented open license.
2. Record the source and license in `sourceAttribution`/`provenance` when adding bundled rules or stat blocks.
3. Do not commit scans, artwork, long quotations, adventure text, setting lore, or Product Identity from proprietary books.
4. Keep fallback responses concise and clearly distinguish open references, user-authored house rules, and unverified model knowledge.
5. Imported content belongs in browser storage and must not be sent to the AI unless it is selected as relevant to the user's current question.
6. When a takedown or provenance concern is raised, disable distribution of the disputed bundled entry until its license is confirmed.
